press 'R' for right wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press 'L' for right wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press 'U' for up wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press 'D' for down wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press 'B' for back wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press 'F' for front wall rotation (90 degrees clockwise).
press ' ' (spacebar) for flipping rotation direction (from clockwise to counter clockwise or vise versa).
press 'Z' for dividing rotation angle by 2.
press 'A' to multiply rotation angle by 2 (until maximum of 180).

Moving while holding left button will rotate the cube: Left to right movement will rotate the cube around global Y axis and up to down
movement will rotate it around global X axis.
Moving while holding right button will move the camera up, down, left or right.
Scroll up and down will move the camera along Z axis (back and forward).
